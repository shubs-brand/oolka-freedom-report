WHAT FREEDOM MEANS

Financial freedom starts with the freedom to ask.

  • To ask a money question without feeling small.
  • To ask it in your own language.
  • To ask it at two in the morning.
  • To ask it with a screenshot, when you don’t understand it.
  • And then to have somebody act on it, not just explain it.

Since day one, India has brought these questions to Dhruva, Oolka’s AI. This is what we learnt from what happened next.
